Assistive navigation

Guide Pup helps blind and low-vision users move with spoken guidance.

Camera frames are sent to the Guide Pup backend for analysis, may be processed by third-party AI providers, and are paired with anonymous device/session bootstrap state. Optional hands-free voice commands use the microphone and iOS speech recognition for a bounded command set. The app may use optional crash reporting.
