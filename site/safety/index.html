Assistive guidance

Guide Pup helps with orientation, not guaranteed hazard detection or emergency response.

The app can return STOP when a scene is unclear, confidence is low, or a hazard is detected. That is a cue to pause, reorient, and wait for clearer guidance before moving again.

When STOP appears

Pause, reorient the phone, and do not treat the output as proof that the path is safe.

What the app is not

It is not a guarantee that every curb, stair, drop-off, obstacle, or environmental hazard will be detected.

Emergency guidance

If there is immediate danger, stop using the app and contact local emergency services or nearby people directly.
